### 5. **Risk Tolerance & Abort Conditions**

**Master Plan (Aug 26):**
- Defines "do not cross" boundaries (e.g., "no Step-2 coding until A5 PASS")
- Emphasizes workstream isolation and gates
- Tolerates longer iterations (weeks per gate)

**Comprehensive Plan (Aug 28):**
- Defines hard abort conditions (pre-specified failure triggers)
- Emphasizes monthly rebalance risk control
- Tolerates 5 weeks total (no re-iterations on preregistration)

**Key Difference:**
- Master plan: Prevents uncontrolled scope creep across teams
- Comprehensive plan: Prevents endless tuning within Item 3b
