# Brain Research Lab — Version 7 Earlier Decision

Version 6 diagnostics found that selected equities rose about 0.20% overnight,
but Version 6 entered at the next morning's open and therefore paid for a move it
did not capture. Version 7 changes one item only.

## Frozen change

- Make the ranking decision after the completed hourly candle beginning at 13:15
  and ending at 14:15, instead of after the 14:15–15:15 candle.
- Hypothetical entry remains the next hourly open, now the 14:15 boundary on the
  same trading day.
- The volume feature compares the current 13:15 slot with the previous twenty
  13:15 slots.
- All Version 6 features, weights, eligibility, maximum two selections, costs,
  slippage, 18-hour holding period, universe, and reporting remain unchanged.

This is exploratory because the existing history informed the timing change. A
positive result will require future shadow validation; it is not live approval.
